# The World on November 20

Editorial AI-generated illustration of the November 20, 2026 news cycle.

Run `python main.py` to copy the included image to `/output/entry.png`. No network or dependencies required.

Sources: Reuters and Associated Press reporting on the Kyiv strikes, Shenzhen APEC summit, AI market selloff, Middle East, COP31 negotiations and the ICJ Rohingya ruling. The summit and ruling occurred November 19 and remained in the November 20 news cycle. The artwork is illustrative, not documentary photography.

The actual AI session transcript must be submitted separately as required by make.fun.
